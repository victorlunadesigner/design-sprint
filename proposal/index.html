Overview

What this sprint is

A focused, async four-week engagement to design one complete, shippable user flow — from research inputs to a high-fidelity prototype ready for development or user testing.

What you get

  • One user flow designed end-to-end with wireframes, UI, and interactive prototype
  • Validated screen inventory so nothing important is missed in future phases
  • Dev-ready handoff with specs, states, and a documented flow map

What this is not

  • A full product redesign or complete screen library
  • A design system build or brand identity project
  • Multiple flows in a single sprint
After the sprint: Additional user flows are delivered through a monthly retainer — one sprint cycle per flow, at a reduced rate.

How we'll work

  • Async-first — Loom updates, written feedback, shared Figma files
  • Two feedback rounds per week, max
  • 48-hour response window on your side to keep the timeline on track
  • Single decision-maker — or one named tie-breaker

Delivery

Four-week timeline

Each week has a clear focus and deliverable. Your intake responses feed directly into Week 1.

Week 1

Align & structure

Review intake, validate flow map, resolve open questions async.

Deliverable: Validated flow map, screen list, questions doc

Week 2

Wireframes

Lo-fi wireframes for the full flow. You review and comment within 48h.

Deliverable: Wireframes for every step in the flow

Week 3

UI & interaction

High-fidelity screens with key states: empty, loading, error, success.

Deliverable: Hi-fi screens + interactive prototype

Week 4

Polish & handoff

Final refinements, annotated specs, and dev-ready export.

Deliverable: Prototype link, specs, flow doc, retainer roadmap
